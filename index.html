General IEC IDMT Formula

t = TMS × b / ( (I / Ip)a − 1 )

where t = operate time (s), TMS = time multiplier setting,
I = fault current, Ip = pickup current.

Definite Time (DT) Stage

t = td     (for I ≥ Ipickup)

The DT stage overrides all IDMT stages above its pickup current.
The effective operate time is taken from the drawn (effective) curve.
